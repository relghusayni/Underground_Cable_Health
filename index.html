About this use case

AI-assisted health monitoring for cable networks in Saudi Arabia

Problem statement

cables and joints in Saudi Arabia can degrade before failure, while early warning signs are often difficult to distinguish from heavy load, weather effects, or faulty sensors.

AI detects abnormal heating and insulation degradation, filters false alarms, and uses agentic AI to prioritize issues, explain the evidence, and recommend actions—helping prevent circuit failures, service disruptions, and costly maintenance.

How we use AI

AI is applied across four layers:

  1. Machine learning to detect abnormalities.
    Models learn the normal behaviour of each cable and joint. Cable temperatures are predicted based on load, soil and weather conditions, while partial-discharge patterns are monitored for abnormal changes.
  2. Generative AI to explain.
    Each detected issue is translated into a clear diagnosis covering the likely cause, supporting evidence, risk and recommended action.
  3. Agentic AI to assist engineers.
    • Briefing Agent: ranks the most important issues and recommends the next action.
    • Analyst Agent: lets engineers ask questions such as “Which circuits are degraded and above 85% load?” or “Why is C-04 flagged?”, with links to the supporting evidence.
  4. Workflow automation to act.
    Once an issue is confirmed, the solution can trigger the appropriate workflow—such as creating a maintenance work order, assigning it to the relevant team, routing it for approval, and tracking the issue through resolution.
